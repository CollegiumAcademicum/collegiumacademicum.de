<p style="text-align: justify">
From German colonialism and genocide to neo-colonial exploitation: on generations of resistance by the Ovaherero and Nama peoples, the struggle for resources, self-determination and climate justice, and the question of what reconciliation and reparations mean in the wake of genocide.</p>

<p style="text-align: justify">
We can look forward to a range of engaging talks and workshops led by activists, artists and academics. Above all, however, we of course want to listen to the voices from Namibia itself.</p>


<b>When: 12-14 FEBRUARY 2027<br>
Where: COLLEGIUM ACADEMICUM, HEIDELBERG</b>
